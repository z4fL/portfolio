## Publication section

- **Mode:** Read. A recruiter or professional contact arrives after project evidence and quickly understands that this is peer-reviewed research, with the option to inspect the paper.
- **Proof and actions:** One verified JAIC publication, the supplied PDF, and the DOI `10.30871/jaic.v9i6.11658`. Actions are Preview, Download PDF, and Read on DOI; the CV shares the modal viewer but exposes only Preview and Download.
- **Direction:** Extend the existing Developer's Workbench system. Keep the entry concise, flat, square, and border-led; distinguish it from a project card through citation-first hierarchy and research metadata.
- **Interaction:** `react-pdf` renders page one in an accessible in-page modal. The card itself carries the full title, concise research summary, and compact methods labels.
- **Scope:** Add one section after Projects plus reusable PDF viewer behavior. Preserve current projects, copy, navigation, and layout outside the requested additions.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A single peer-reviewed paper reads as a citation ledger entry, not a portfolio project card.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight and graphite surfaces, warm-paper type, restrained coral signals, General Sans prose, Fira Code for journal and method metadata, and flat square borders.

STORY: Visitors see the publication's scholarly context, learn the concrete modelling question and result, then choose an appropriately deep reading action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: After the project grid, the new section title anchors a full-width, border-led publication record: bibliographic detail and title lead left, concise evidence and three actions follow, with methods grouped as compact metadata rather than decorative badges.

FORM: Confirmed local extension of the portfolio; no concept seed is required because the section's information architecture and interaction were approved in the shape brief.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
